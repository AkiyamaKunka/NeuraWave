# NeuraWave privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

NeuraWave (for iPhone and Mac) collects no data.

- No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
- The app makes no network connections.
- Your choices (preset, tone style, volume, timer, program) are stored only on
  your device, in the app's own settings, and are deleted when you delete the
  app.
- Nothing is shared with the developer or any third party.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/AkiyamaKunka/NeuraWave/issues.
